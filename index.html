Questions

What is Ribbon Rush?

Ribbon Rush is a free, cozy block puzzle game for iPhone and Android by the independent studio Frame Labs. You place satin ribbon pieces on an 8×8 board to wrap gift boxes, earn stars, collect charms and unlock themed worlds.

How do you play?

Drag ribbon pieces onto the board. Fill every square of the dashed ring around a gift to wrap it. Full rows and columns also clear. The game ends when none of your three pieces fits.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free with ads. You can buy stars or remove the ads between games, but you never have to.

When does it come out?

Ribbon Rush is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

What are charms and themes?

Charms are small collectibles that drop from wrapped gifts. Each album has 12. Finishing an album unlocks its theme, a new world for your board.

Do I need the internet?

No, you can play offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.
