Privacy policy

Ribbon Rush · Last updated: 30 September 2026

What the game keeps

Your scores, stars, charms, settings and saved game are stored only on your device. There are no accounts, and we do not collect your name, email or contacts.

Ads

Ribbon Rush is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the game asks for Apple's permission to track. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalized.

Sharing

When you share a score or album card, the image goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

Ribbon Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
